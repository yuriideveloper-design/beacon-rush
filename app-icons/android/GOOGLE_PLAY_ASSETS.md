# Google Play publication assets

These assets were generated locally from an image generated by the user's Codex session from the original application request:
A glowing emergency flare rising above a coastal beacon and ship silhouette, capturing Beacon Rush's urgent night signaling challenge.

- `play_store_icon_512.png`: 512×512 32-bit RGBA Google Play listing icon (maximum 1 MB).
- `feature_graphic_1024x500.png`: 1024×500 24-bit RGB feature graphic without transparency.
- `launcher_icon_1024.png` (Expo) or `ic_launcher_legacy_192.png` (Flutter/Kotlin): launcher source.
- `ic_launcher_foreground_432.png`: 108dp adaptive foreground at xxxhdpi.
- `ic_launcher_monochrome_432.png`: Android themed-icon monochrome layer.

Review the generated artwork before publishing. Store-listing graphics are not uploaded to Play Console
automatically. Also provide screenshots, localized listing text, privacy policy, and accurate Play Console
Data safety/content declarations.
